# Progressive cover derivatives

These JPEGs are resized/encoded derivatives of the approved, unchanged
`assets/brand/mdaai-guardian-cover.webp` plate, cover by E.M.K. The inherited
cover attribution and publication terms remain applicable; no new blanket
reuse license is granted. Preserve the existing homepage cover caption.

Generator: `website/cover.py`, Pillow 12.1.0, quality 95, 4:4:4 chroma,
optimized progressive JPEG with LANCZOS resizing and no crop, recoloring,
sharpening, composition changes, or upscaling. `inventory.json` records the
master digest, encoder version, dimensions, content hashes and quality evidence.
